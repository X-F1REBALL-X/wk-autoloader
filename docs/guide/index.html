Jailbreak, then open Elf Launcher.

WK Autoloader is a home screen tile and browser page for a jailbroken PS5. It runs the exploit chain, then opens Elf Launcher so your payloads are one press away.

Free and open source. Developed by X-F1REBALL-X.

What it is

A small ELF that installs a home screen tile and serves the WK Autoloader page on port 1022. Open it from the tile, from the PS5 browser, or from your phone or PC on the same network.

Home tile

Installs under Games. One press opens the page, even offline after the first cache.

Chains for every FW

Auto picks the best chain. Or choose umtx2, Relapse, or Poops. Unsupported chains stay greyed out.

Opens Elf Launcher

After a successful jailbreak it opens Elf Launcher Hybrid on port 1000, and sends the bundled tip when needed.

Install

Four steps, then you can jailbreak from the home screen.

  1. Soft jailbreak onceStart elfldr on port 9021.
  2. Send the installerSend wk-autoloader.elf from the latest release to the console. Wait for the home icon toast.
  3. Open the pageUse the home tile, or open http://PS5_IP:1022 from your phone or PC.
  4. StartPick a chain (or leave Auto), set the delay if you want, then press Start. After the jailbreak, Elf Launcher opens.

What's new in 1.1.0

A finer delay slider, ten languages, and Elf Launcher 2.0.0 bundled.

Timing

  • Delay slider from 0.5 to 5 seconds, in 0.5 steps, with a half-second countdown.
  • Start and Cancel share one button. During the countdown it shows Cancel and the remaining seconds.
  • Jailbreak starts right after the countdown, with only a short probe for Elf Launcher.

Page and languages

  • Ten languages to choose from: English, Arabic, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Chinese, Italian.

Package

  • Bundled Elf Launcher tip updated to the released 2.0.0 build.
  • Installer toasts are shorter. Home icon toast only on first install.
